ELECTRONIKA8

This Soviet research institute is allergic to lab assistants, two of them went to the next building for a soldering iron before you and are still listed as being on a business trip. But your lab coat has been issued and your mugshot is already stamped on your pass, so solder away and don't ask unnecessary questions. There are no unnecessary questions here, only unnecessary lab assistants.

Comrade lab assistant!

Congratulations, you're on the staff, and someone has already signed the safety register for you so you don't get distracted from work. The canteen has even set aside a cutlet in your name, only you'll get it after the quarterly report. Even a philology trainee could handle the first job... rumour has it one did, and was transferred to the stores to be in charge of every light switch in the building. Build your circuits, and if everything goes right you get the ACCEPTED stamp, and if not... well, the lab always smells of something burnt.

Real physics

  • The circuits run on a real electronics simulator, so Ohm's law works, and you can't repeal it at a party meeting, we checked.
  • Parts heat up and burn, a cheap resistor will go up in smoke, while a good one first blushes like a freshman at a thesis defence, but burnt parts still come out of your pocket, and the mark on the board stays too.
  • You'll be issued a multimeter and an oscilloscope, but what they're for, we don't know. The head of the lab doesn't know either, so they're all covered in dust.

Deeper into the institute

  • 94 levels, and how it all ends is up to you alone.
  • Your own chips. You can build a gate from transistors, send it to the factory and get a chip back, but Soviet factories sometimes mix up the pins... so be careful.
  • Everybody needs money... good parts cost more, the flea market is cheaper, but nothing is labelled, and whether you bought a transistor or a greatcoat button you'll only find out when the power goes on.
  • A career. Lab assistants don't stay lab assistants for long here, and where they go afterwards, we don't know.

The institute around you

  • A room for every chapter, Pravda on the desk and a radio playing real music by someone who lived through those times.
  • A sandbox workshop full of parts, where you can solder whatever you like, and it's the only place in the institute where nobody's watching you, although...
  • There are badges and awards in little boxes too, ten of them secret.
  • No knowledge of electronics needed, the game teaches you as you go, and after all, the current head of the lab was once sent to the institute as a lab assistant too.

Demo

The first two chapters, 22 levels of current, lamps and Ohm's law, just enough to see what you've got yourself into but not quite enough to make a run for it, and your saves carry over to the full game.

Privacy

ELECTRONIKA8 collects and sends no data about you. The game has no analytics, no ads and no accounts. Your progress is kept in a file on your computer (%APPDATA%\Electronika8), and Steam Cloud copies it between your machines under Steam's own terms. Achievements and your status in the friends list go through the Steam client. Questions go to @dalerank.
